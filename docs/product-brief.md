# Product Brief

## Service Name
Lahaph Official Website

## Purpose
Introduce Lahaph as a trusted nonprofit arts organization and education community, and convert parent visitors into inquiries.

## Core Value
- Trust
- Professionalism
- Program clarity
- Real outcomes through performances and education

## Primary Users
Parents of children with developmental disabilities

## Main User Jobs
- Understand who Lahaph is
- Explore programs and performances
- Decide whether the organization is trustworthy
- Contact Lahaph for inquiry

## Success Metrics
- Inquiry form submissions
- Page views on Academy / Art College
- Time spent on Musical pages
- Return visits to Board / Contents
