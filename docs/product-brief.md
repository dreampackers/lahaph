# Product Brief

## Project Name
Lahaph Official Website

## Service Type
Official nonprofit/cultural organization website

## Purpose
This website introduces Lahaph as:
- a nonprofit arts organization
- a developmental disability arts community
- an education and performance platform

The website should help visitors:
- understand the organization
- explore performances and programs
- view educational offerings
- contact Lahaph

## Primary Target User
Parents of children with developmental disabilities

## Secondary Users
- families
- supporters
- partner organizations
- cultural and educational professionals

## Core Value Proposition
Lahaph is a trusted organization that supports the growth of developmental disability artists through performance, education, and community.

## Main User Needs
- understand what Lahaph is
- see the organization's philosophy and history
- review educational programs
- explore musical works and achievements
- access contact information
- evaluate trustworthiness through public information

## Success Indicators
- increase inquiries
- increase visits to Academy and Art College pages
- increase visits to Musical pages
- improve trust through Board and organization pages
